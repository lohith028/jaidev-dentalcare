How Often Should You Visit a Dentist? A Bengaluru Dentist Explains

By Dr. M. Premkumar, BDS · Consultant Dental Surgeon · 15 April 2025 · 4 min read

If you're only seeing a dentist when your tooth aches, you're already in reactive mode — and that usually means more extensive (and expensive) treatment. In my practice at Electronic City, I often meet patients who haven't had a check-up in years.

The Standard Recommendation: Every 6 Months

For most children and adults, the golden rule is to visit your dental clinic in Bengaluru twice a year. This isn't just a random number; it's based on how quickly plaque and tartar can accumulate, even with good brushing habits.

Why Twice a Year?

  • Early Detection: We can spot cavities while they're tiny, often requiring only a simple filling.
  • Gum Health: Regular scaling prevents gingivitis from turning into serious gum disease.
  • Oral Cancer Screening: Every routine check-up at Jaidev Dental includes a quick screen for signs of oral cancer.
