5 Early Signs of Gum Disease You Shouldn't Ignore in Bengaluru

By Dr. M. Premkumar, BDS · 2 March 2025 · 5 min read

Gum disease is one of the leading causes of tooth loss in adults worldwide — and most people don't know they have it until it's well advanced. In our Bengaluru dental clinic, we focus on early detection to prevent surgical interventions later.

Sign 1: Bleeding Gums

If your gums bleed when you brush or floss, it's not normal. It's often the first sign of inflammation caused by plaque build-up along the gum line.

Sign 2: Red or Swollen Gums

Healthy gums are firm and pale pink. If they look red, puffy, or feel tender to the touch, your body is fighting a localized infection.

Sign 3: Persistent Bad Breath

Chronic bad breath that doesn't go away after brushing is often caused by bacteria living in "pockets" between your teeth and gums.
