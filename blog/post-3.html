Teeth Whitening vs. Cleaning: What's the Difference?

By Dr. M. Premkumar, BDS · 10 February 2025 · 3 min read

Patients ask us this question every week — and the confusion is completely understandable. While both involve making your teeth look better, they serve very different purposes.

What Is a Professional Dental Cleaning?

A cleaning (scaling) is a medical necessity. Its primary goal is health. We remove plaque and tartar that can cause gum disease and tooth decay. It also removes surface stains from coffee or tea, but it doesn't change the actual color of your teeth.

What Is Teeth Whitening?

Whitening is a cosmetic procedure. We use safe, professional-grade bleaching agents to break down deep stains within the enamel, brightening your smile by several shades.
