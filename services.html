Dental Implants

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed into your jawbone to serve as an artificial tooth root. It's the most natural-looking and long-lasting solution for missing teeth. Implants prevent bone loss and keep adjacent teeth from shifting. At Jaidev Dental Care, we use precision techniques for predictable, beautiful results.

Who needs this?
  • Missing one or more teeth
  • Unhappy with dentures or bridges

Dental Crowns & Bridges

A crown ('cap') completely covers a damaged or weakened tooth to restore its shape, strength, and appearance. Bridges fill the gap left by one or more missing teeth, anchoring to adjacent teeth. We offer porcelain and zirconia crowns that match your natural tooth colour exactly.

Who needs this?
  • Cracked, broken, or heavily filled tooth
  • Gap from a missing tooth

Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Root canal treatment removes infected or damaged pulp from inside the tooth, relieving severe toothache and saving the natural tooth. Thanks to modern anaesthesia, RCT at our clinic is virtually painless and often completed in a single sitting.

Who needs this?
  • Severe or persistent toothache
  • Sensitivity to hot or cold

Orthodontic Braces & Aligners
