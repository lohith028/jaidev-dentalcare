Meet Dr. M. Premkumar — Dental Surgeon in Electronic City

Dr. M. Premkumar, B.D.S.

Consultant Dental Surgeon

Dr. M. Premkumar is the founder and lead dental surgeon at Jaidev Multispeciality Dental Care in Electronic City, Bengaluru. With over a decade of hands-on experience, he specialises in dental implants, root canal treatments, cosmetic dentistry, and orthodontics.

Dr. Premkumar is known for his calm, patient-first approach — taking the time to explain each procedure clearly so patients never feel anxious or confused. He has treated thousands of patients across all age groups in the Electronic City community.

"Every smile tells a story. My job is to make sure yours is a great one." — Dr. M. Premkumar

Our Clinic — Built on Trust, Care, and Precision

Jaidev Multispeciality Dental Care was established to serve the growing community of Electronic City, Bengaluru. We saw a need for a neighbourhood dental clinic that combines specialist-level expertise with genuine warmth. Today, we are proud to serve over 5,000 patients and their families.

Our clinic is equipped with modern dental technology, including digital X-rays and rotary root canal instruments, to ensure precise, comfortable, and efficient treatments.
